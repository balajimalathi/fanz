# Privacy Policy

## Introduction

This Privacy Policy (hereinafter "Policy") explains how **Exclusivz** (hereinafter "we," "us," "our," or "Company") collects, uses, discloses, and otherwise processes personal data in connection with our website **www.exclusivz.com**, our mobile applications, and all associated services, features, content, and products (collectively, the "Platform").

This Policy applies to all individuals who use the Platform, including content creators ("Creators"), fans and subscribers ("Subscribers" or "Fans"), and individuals who appear in user-generated content ("Content Collaborators").

We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience on our Platform. Please read this Policy carefully. By accessing or using the Platform, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Policy and agree to the collection, use, and disclosure of your personal data as outlined herein.

**If you do not agree with our privacy practices, please do not use the Platform.**

- **Retention**: Duration of your account + retention policy

- **Retention**: As required by tax laws (typically 6-7 years in India)

#### Identity Verification Documents
- **Retention**: Duration of account + 2 years post-closure
- **Includes**: Government ID copies, selfies, verification results

#### Communication Records
- **Retention**: Duration of account + 1 year post-closure
- **Includes**: Messages, support tickets, chat history
- **Use**: Dispute resolution and customer service

#### Usage and Analytics Data
- **Retention**: 24 months
- **Includes**: Clickstream data, device information, usage patterns
- **Anonymized**: After 12 months for aggregate analytics

#### Call Metadata
- **Retention**: 1 year
- **Includes**: Date, time, duration, participants, quality metrics
- **Note**: Actual call content is NOT recorded or stored

#### Cookies and Tracking Data
- **Retention**: As per cookie policy (typically 1-24 months)
- **Renewal**: Refreshed on each Platform visit

#### Legal Hold Data
- **Retention**: As required by law or pending legal proceedings
- **Includes**: Data relevant to investigations, disputes, or litigation
